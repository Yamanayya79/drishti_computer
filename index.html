About the institute

Practical learning for a digital world.

Drishti Computer Education, associated with A9 Solutions, is a computer training and skill development centre in Dharwad, Karnataka. We aim to help learners build confidence through practical computer education, technical training and useful digital skills.

✓ Beginner-friendly learning
✓ Practical skill development
✓ Course guidance
✓ Student support

Our learning approach

We help students understand concepts, practise with tools and develop skills they can use in education, office work and technology-related careers.

Who can enquire?
Students, beginners, job seekers, working professionals and anyone interested in computer skills.
